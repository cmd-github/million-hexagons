Where the deck was still right

Keep the number prominent — it genuinely is the only part that survives a screenshot or a re-post. Keep views and visits off the card. Keep the anonymous state as a real layout rather than a stripped-down one. And ship the Open Graph metadata alongside this, or the best-converting path still leaks.

The disagreement is narrow: a memento is something you keep, and an invitation is something you send. This needs to be the second one.
