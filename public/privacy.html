Privacy information

Staging draft — not approved for live sales. This describes the tested product at a high level. Final company details, provider locations and retention periods must be verified before publication.

Who runs the service

Birdcage Tech Ltd operates Million Hexagons. For privacy requests, email support@birdcagetech.com. Verified company and postal details will be added before live sales.

Accounts and purchases

We use an email address to send a sign-in link and recover access to placements. We store orders, assigned cell IDs, placement versions, payment status and customer support records so we can provide the service, resolve problems and meet applicable obligations. Stripe processes checkout and payment information. We do not ask you to enter card details into our own forms.

Public placements

Your chosen placement name, artwork, optional description and link, claim date and assigned cell count can appear publicly, including in share previews. Do not include personal details you do not want public. A placement link can remain accessible while the claim continues; contact us to correct or hide content. Search engines and people who copied a link may retain older versions for a time.

Storage and optional measurement

Necessary browser storage supports sign-in, draft recovery, artwork loading and your privacy choice. Optional measurement records globe and placement interactions and uses a browser session identifier for deduplication only after you choose “Allow measurement”. “No thanks” leaves this measurement off. You can reopen Privacy choices from the globe. Payment and publication records still operate when measurement is off.

Providers and rights

The staging service uses Firebase for accounts and authoritative records, Cloudflare for delivery, Stripe for payment and browser storage for session work. We will publish verified processor, transfer and retention details before live sales. You can ask about access, correction, deletion or objection by emailing support@birdcagetech.com. Some order and ownership records may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve a continuing claim.
